Bills View only

Bills for representative and department offices, and the payments they record. Offices pay online (Razorpay, when it is switched on) or by UPI or bank transfer to the details in Prices & site details.

The amount comes from the published prices on the day the bill is made, so every party pays the same; it can't be typed in. Before you confirm a payment, find it in the bank statement (reference, amount and date).
