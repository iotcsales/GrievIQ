Government access View only

State and district officials who may open GrievIQ's government dashboard, what they opened, and the representatives' offices that chose to share their own figures.

The rules: access only on a letter from the state government, to an official email (gov.in or nic.in); reviewed every year. Officials see government departments in full, but representatives' offices only as totals for each district, unless an office chooses to share. No citizen's name, phone number or complaint text is ever shown.

The two dashboards: State Dashboard (grieviq.in/state) (demo) · National Dashboard (grieviq.in/national) (demo)
