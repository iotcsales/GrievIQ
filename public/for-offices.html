Complaints arrive from everywhere. Proof of work is hard to show.

Petitions come on paper, by phone and on WhatsApp — to you, your staff and your personal number. Some get lost. A department says the job is done, but nobody checks on the ground. When a citizen asks "what happened?", there is no record to show.

How it works

  1. A citizen files a complaint with a photo and location — free, in Hindi or English — and gets a reference number.
  2. Your office gets an alert, acknowledges the complaint and assigns it to a team member.
  3. The department replies: scheduled, started, done, not ours, or can't do. Your field worker checks on site and uploads a location-checked photo.
  4. The citizen confirms and rates the result. If a time limit passes at any level, the complaint moves up automatically.

The levels a complaint moves through:

  1. Ward or village
  2. Mayor
  3. MLA
  4. MP

What your office gets

For a representative's office

  • Every complaint numbered and tracked on one dashboard.
  • Your team, not your personal phone: office manager, field worker and assistant roles.
  • Push alerts and assignments, so nothing waits unseen.
  • Reports and charts you can download and share.
  • A record of your work that cannot be altered.

For a government department office

  • Only the complaints that are yours, with location and photo.
  • Reply in one tap — and say "not ours" when it isn't.
  • Location-checked photos show the work was done on the ground.
  • Performance figures for your office — not ranked against others during the pilot.
  • Clear, written rules for officers, agreed in a signed agreement.

What your citizens get

Free filing in Hindi or English, a reference number, status updates, a published time limit at every level, and a say in whether the job was really done. Their phone number stays hidden.
