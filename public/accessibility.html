Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use GrievIQ, including people with disabilities and people on older phones or slow connections. We aim to meet India's accessibility standard IS 17802 and the international WCAG 2.1 guidelines at level AA.

What you can do

  • Use the whole site with a keyboard alone, starting with the “Skip to main content” link.
  • Use a screen reader: form fields have labels, and photos have descriptions.
  • Switch between English and Hindi on any page.
  • Zoom text up to 200% without losing content.
  • Choose your area from a list instead of using the map.
  • Use the site with animations reduced, if your device asks for that.

Known limitations

  • The map on the complaint page can be hard to use with a screen reader. You can choose your area from the list and type a landmark instead.
  • PDF reports downloaded from the representatives' console may not be fully readable by screen readers.

How we check

We test pages with a keyboard and a screen reader while building them. An independent accessibility audit is planned.

Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, write to support@grieviq.in with “Accessibility” in the subject and the page you were on. We reply within 7 days, and can help you file your complaint another way in the meantime.
