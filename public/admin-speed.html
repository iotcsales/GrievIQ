How this is measured

About 1 in every 20 requests to GrievIQ's servers records how long it took. No person, address or complaint is recorded. Every hour the figures are worked out for each part of GrievIQ and kept for 30 days. If a part is slow for 2 hours in a row, every super admin gets one email that day.
