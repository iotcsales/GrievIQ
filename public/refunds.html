Refunds and cancellation

This policy is for representatives' offices and department offices that pay for GrievIQ, and for Community helpers. Citizens never pay to file or follow a complaint.

Loading…

Community helpers' monthly fee

  • Refused for a reason that isn't your fault (for example, an unclear photo or a ward GrievIQ can't serve yet): the first month is refunded in full.
  • You withdraw your application before the decision: the first month is refunded in full.
  • Refused because the documents were forged or altered: no refund.
  • Removed for misuse: no refund for the current month. Stopping on your own: part-months are not refunded.
  • Refunds go back to the account you paid from, usually within 5–7 working days.

To ask for a refund

Write to support@grieviq.in with the bill number (such as GIQ-PAY-000001) and the payment reference.
