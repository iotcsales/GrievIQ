Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

GrievIQ is an independent platform, not officially operated by any government authority. See our About us page for details.

What we collect

When you file a grievance, we collect your name, phone number, and (optionally) email address, together with your grievance description, photos, and location. If you use email-based status verification, we send a one-time code to that email to confirm it's you.

How we use it

We use your information solely to:

  • Route your grievance to the correct elected representative for your ward
  • Notify you of status updates and escalations
  • Verify your identity when you check on a case

We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Data retention

Grievance records (tracking number, description, status history) are retained for 3 years from filing, or 1 year after resolution, whichever is longer. One-time verification codes are deleted immediately after use or upon expiry (10 minutes). Your email or phone number is retained only as long as the associated grievance record.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, subject to our legitimate need to maintain grievance records for accountability purposes. To make such a request, contact us at support@grieviq.in.

Who operates GrievIQ

GrievIQ is built and operated by IOTC (Indus Overseas Tech. Corp.). For any privacy-related questions, reach us at support@grieviq.in.
