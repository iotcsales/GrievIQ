Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

GrievIQ is an independent platform, not officially operated by any government authority. See our About us page for details.

What we collect, and why

  • Your mobile number (required): kept with your complaint so it can be identified if you contact us about it.
  • Your email address (optional): to send you a one-time code when you track your reports online, and to email you when your case is marked resolved.
  • What you report: the type of problem, your description, your ward, any location details you give, and any photos you attach. We use these to send your complaint to the right elected representative and show them the problem.
  • A representative's name or phone number (optional, only if you tell us): used only to check and update our records of public representatives.

We don't ask for your name.

How we use it

We use your information only to:

  • Send your complaint to the correct elected representative for your ward, and to each higher level if it isn't dealt with in time
  • Email you when your case is marked resolved, so you can confirm it's fixed or tell us it isn't
  • Confirm it's you when you track your reports
  • Let GrievIQ staff oversee how complaints are handled, for example by reviewing overdue or disputed cases

We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Who sees your complaint

  • Your representatives: the ward representative and, if the case moves up, each level it reaches, see your description, location and photos. They do not see your mobile number or email address.
  • GrievIQ staff: see your complaint to oversee how it's handled. Your mobile number and email are partly hidden from them, and every time a staff member opens a case, it is recorded.
  • Photos: photos are stored at unlisted web addresses. Anyone who has a photo's exact link can open it, so please don't include anything in a photo that you wouldn't want seen.

Stored on your device

When you file a report, its tracking number is saved in your browser on that device, so the home page can show how many reports you've filed there. Your language choice is saved the same way. This stays on your device and we can't see it. You can remove the tracking numbers at any time with "Forget reports on this device" on the home page.

How long we keep it

Complaint records (tracking number, description, status history) are kept for 3 years from filing, or 1 year after resolution, whichever is longer. Your mobile number and email are kept only as long as the complaint they belong to. One-time codes expire after 10 minutes and work only once: a code is wiped as soon as it's used, and all verification records are deleted within a day.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, subject to our legitimate need to keep complaint records for accountability. You can also withdraw your consent to us using your data at any time; this doesn't affect anything already done before you withdrew it. To do any of these, email us at support@grieviq.in.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Who operates GrievIQ

GrievIQ is built and operated by IOTC (Indus Overseas Tech. Corp.). For any privacy-related questions, reach us at support@grieviq.in.
