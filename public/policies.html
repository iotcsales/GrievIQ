Website policies

These policies cover how you may use and link to GrievIQ's content. How we handle your data is in the Privacy policy, and the rules for using the service are in the Terms of use.

Copyright policy

You may reproduce content from GrievIQ free of charge, as long as it is reproduced accurately, is not used in a misleading or derogatory way, and GrievIQ is named as the source. This does not cover content owned by others, such as map data from Google, which belongs to its owners.

Hyperlinking policy

Links to GrievIQ: you don't need our permission to link to our pages, but please don't show them inside frames on your own website.

Links from GrievIQ: we link to other websites, such as government portals, for your convenience. We are not responsible for their content or availability, and a link does not mean we endorse them.

Disclaimer

GrievIQ is an independent platform, not a government service. We send complaints to representatives' public contact details and show their progress, but we cannot guarantee that a problem will be resolved. We check the information on this site regularly, but it may contain errors; please tell us if you find one.

Content review and archiving

We review the content of these pages at least every six months. Outdated information is removed or marked as archived.

Security

We test GrievIQ regularly for security problems. If you find one, please tell us at security@grieviq.in.
