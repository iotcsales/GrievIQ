Change of representative View only

When a ward's, village's, mayor's, MLA's or MP's seat passes to someone else, record it here: a new representative, an administrator, or a vacant seat.

The rules: record a change only on official evidence (the election result, the government order, or the resignation accepted), with its number or link. A data entry operator asks; a super or operations admin approves, never their own request, or applies it directly. The office's complaints and records stay with the office.
