Community helpers

Applications to check and approve, helpers, appeals and refunds.

One staff member checks (ID photo, selfie and a video call); a different senior staff member approves. Every time an ID photo or selfie is opened, it is recorded in the helper's history; the photos are deleted 30 days after the decision. The applicant sees the reason for a refusal, suspension or removal, so write it about the application or conduct only. A helper's name is never shown publicly.
