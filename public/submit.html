      Before you file: how your details are used

      • Mobile number: so your complaint can be identified and, if needed, someone can call you about it. Representatives never see it; GrievIQ staff see it partly hidden.
      • Email (optional): to send you updates and let you confirm the problem is fixed.
      • Your complaint, place and photos: to send it to the right representative and track it until it is resolved.

      We keep these for 3 years from filing, or 1 year after the complaint is closed if that is later, then remove your personal details.

      You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent, by writing to our Grievance Officer at grievance@grieviq.in. Full details: Privacy policy.
